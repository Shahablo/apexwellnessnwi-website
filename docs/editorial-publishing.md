# Editorial publication workflow

Effective September 12, 2026. Target: one useful website article per week, Wednesdays at 9:00 a.m. America/Chicago, after review. This supersedes the older three-articles-per-week proposal; the old calendar remains a topic backlog.

## Explicit draft gate

The GLP-1 expectations/side-effects and weight-loss plateau articles are held with `status: "draft"`. Their existing date fields are planning metadata, not approval or a scheduler. The build already excludes drafts regardless of date. No currently live article has been removed.

Before release, obtain actual owner approval and named clinician review of the final medical copy. Store the approval reference and exact reviewed version in the private publishing ledger outside the public checkout. Recheck sources, clinical claims, launch facts, links, and image rights. Never manufacture a reviewer identity, credential, quote, review date, or approval.

Then set the article to a non-draft publication status and replace planning dates with the actual publication and meaningful modification dates. Build and test, publish through the existing GitHub-to-Cloudflare flow, and verify the production article, blog listing, feed, canonical, and sitemap. Only then distribute a link on social platforms. An elapsed target time must never authorize release.

## Recurring work and review files

The local review queue is `D:\ApexWellness\branding\social\review`. The dated draft packs, editorial calendar, and publication ledger there are operational records, not public website assets. Facebook and Instagram require explicit owner review and approval of the final caption and final media before native scheduling or posting. LinkedIn review/scheduling follows the current owner's instructions and applicable action-time confirmations.

The Codex scheduled workflow prepares the weekly pack, checks approvals and scheduling gaps, and requests a release decision where necessary. It is not equivalent to a native platform post queue. A post is recorded as SCHEDULED only after a platform confirms its date/time; PUBLISHED requires a verified live permalink.

Keep site architecture, existing content, analytics/privacy settings, hosting, credentials, and public contact information unchanged unless an authorized editorial release requires a scoped change.
