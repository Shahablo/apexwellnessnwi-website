Before the Doors Open: Bringing Apex Wellness to Life

Getting Apex Wellness ready has meant making progress in all kinds of ways. Some of it is easy to see: our name on the door, posters in place, and a space that is starting to look like Apex. Some of it happens between those pictures, in pickup arrangements, equipment orders and trips back and forth to the printer.

We wanted to share a little of that part of the story. We are still getting ready at 8550 Broadway, Suite B, in Merrillville. This was a day of practical jobs, a few stubborn obstacles and some genuinely enjoyable introductions around the neighborhood.

By Apex Wellness Editorial Team · Published October 24, 2026

The lettering put up a fight

Scraping off the old window signage was a bigger job than we expected. It was a huge pain, honestly. The photo and video catch the hands-on part better than a polished announcement could: working at the glass, removing old lettering and making room for the Apex name.

There is a lot of planning involved in getting a clinic ready, but sometimes the task directly in front of you is simply a stubborn piece of signage. Seeing the window change gave us one small, visible piece of progress while the rest of the work continued.

The logistics behind the scenes

The other challenge was coordinating things that needed to arrive. Figuring out a refrigerator pickup and sorting out our centrifuge order took some organizing. Those details do not make the most exciting pictures, but they are part of the work behind the space you see here.

A photo can show a room or a box. It cannot show every order detail or pickup arrangement that sits behind it. And arranging equipment is not the same thing as having it installed, checked and ready for care.

A few more trips to FedEx

Getting the posters up involved plenty of back-and-forth to FedEx. After the running around, our posters and QR codes were in place. It was another small win we could actually see.

Those posters give people passing by a way to learn about Apex and follow opening updates while we finish getting ready.

Getting to know the neighborhood

Our favorite part of the day was meeting local business owners and learning about the neighborhood. Getting our own place ready has also given us a chance to get to know the people around us. The conversations were easy; the window was not.

And yes, we discovered some seriously good local donuts. They were a welcome break between scraping, logistics and printer runs.

Come along for the next part

There is still work ahead, and we are excited to bring you along as we get Apex ready. The name on the door feels good; the less visible checks still matter just as much.
