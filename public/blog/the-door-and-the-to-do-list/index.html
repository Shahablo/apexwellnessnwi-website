The door says Apex. The to-do list has other plans.

The Apex Wellness name is on the door in Merrillville now. We would love to tell you that was the moment everything fell neatly into place. In reality, it was one satisfying checkmark on a list that keeps finding ways to grow.

This is Part 1 of our build-out journal: not a grand-opening announcement, and definitely not a tour of a finished clinic. It is the less photogenic, more honest middle.

By Apex Wellness Editorial Team · Published October 24, 2026

In this article

  • A name on the door is the visible part

  • Why the careful parts matter
  • The neighbors are part of the story, too
  • To be continued

A name on the door is the visible part

The old window lettering was not interested in a graceful exit. There was scraping. There was more scraping. At some point, a donut became a perfectly reasonable project-management tool.

We also put up posters and QR codes and got the Apex name onto the door. Those changes are small compared with the whole job, but they matter. A person driving past 8550 Broadway, Suite B, should be able to recognize the place we are preparing and find accurate updates about it.

Then comes the work a photograph cannot show

A clinic needs more than a sign and a good room. We coordinated a refrigerator pickup, worked through a centrifuge order, made trips to FedEx and spoke with Comcast about internet service. Each of those threads still needs to be seen through.

None of that makes a dramatic before-and-after photo. It is the practical work that sits between a concept and a functioning place. The small jobs compete for attention with the big ones, and the list rarely sorts itself.

Why the careful parts matter

A finished-looking door is not a readiness check for a medical clinic. Coordinating a pickup is not the same as having equipment in place and checked. Posters can point to information, but they do not settle staffing, systems or when care can begin. Those pieces deserve more than a hopeful date and a camera angle.

Our planned focus is weight and metabolic care, men’s hormone health, women’s midlife care and hair-loss care. We want to introduce those areas clearly without suggesting visits are available before the people, space and processes are ready. We will share confirmed opening details when we have them.

The neighbors are part of the story, too

We met nearby business owners while working on the space. We are new to this stretch of Merrillville, and getting to know the people around us has been one of the best parts of the process. We traded introductions, listened and picked up some highly unofficial local donut research.

Some days progress looks like a name on a door. Other days it is a phone call, a shipping errand or a question that needs an answer before the next step. Both count. Neither is permission to skip the careful work.

To be continued

We are still preparing to open Apex Wellness at 8550 Broadway, Suite B, Merrillville. For now, follow along or join the Launch List for opening updates. It asks for contact information, not medical history, and signing up does not reserve an appointment.

For this first installment, we are happy to report that the name is on the door and that the donuts did their job. The to-do list, of course, had no comment.
