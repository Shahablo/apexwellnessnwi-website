From request to possible appointment

Know what happens before you submit.

  1. Send a contact request.

    Share only your name and email. Do not submit symptoms, diagnoses, medications, laboratory values, or insurance information.

  2. Review verified launch details.

    Apex may email you when clinician, location or visit modality, pricing, policies, and consultation availability are confirmed.

  3. Decide whether to schedule.

    If an appropriate time is available, you can decide whether to book after reviewing the applicable details and terms.

  4. Use the secure clinical process.

    Clinical history and medical information will be collected only through the appropriate intake process after an appointment is confirmed.

Our approach

The plan starts with the person, not a product.

Apex is preparing a care model in which history, concerns, goals, risks, and appropriate testing are considered before a plan is written. Medication is discussed only when clinically appropriate, and outcomes are never guaranteed.

Why Apex

What physician management is intended to mean here.

  • A physician reviews each evaluation, relevant result, and written clinical plan.
  • Dosing, monitoring, and referral decisions rely on clinical judgment rather than an automatic protocol.
  • The practice stays focused on three care areas with clear fees and exclusions.
  • Progress is reviewed using symptoms, measurements, laboratory data when relevant, function, adherence, and side effects.
  • When another clinician is the better destination, the plan is to say so clearly.
