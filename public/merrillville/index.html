Before planning a visit

Is the clinic open for appointments or walk-ins?

Not yet. Apex is coming soon, and scheduling is not open. The Launch List is for updates; it does not reserve a visit or establish care.

Will Apex offer telehealth at launch?

No. Visits are planned to be in person at launch. Join the Launch List for opening updates; joining does not book an appointment.

Can I ask about the location or access?

Call (219) 207-2456 or email hello@apexwellnessnwi.com with a general location or accessibility question. Please keep medical details out of ordinary email and the Launch List form.

Where can I find fees and visit details?

The Pricing and How It Works pages explain the planned process. Final fees, clinician details and appointment availability will be shared before scheduling opens.
